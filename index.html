About Us

About Ahluwalia Farm

Ahluwalia Farm is dedicated to producing premium natural food products with a commitment to quality, authenticity, and responsible farming. We specialize in raw & unprocessed honey and fresh cage-free eggs, bringing together traditional farming values with scientifically recommended agricultural practices.

Our team and partner farmers have received training from Krishi Vigyan Kendra (KVK) in beekeeping and poultry management. We follow recommended farming practices to maintain quality, hygiene, and consistency across our operations.

Every product reflects our commitment to freshness, transparency, and customer trust.

Poultry

Fresh Cage-Free Eggs

Our eggs are produced by BV 380 laying hens raised in a cage-free farming system.

We work with a network of eight poultry farms around the Amritsar region of Punjab. Every farm is operated by farmers trained through Krishi Vigyan Kendra (KVK), following recommended poultry management practices.

  • Scientifically planned shed orientation
  • Recommended bird space
  • Strict biosecurity
  • Quality feed management
  • Responsible flock management
  • Hygienic egg collection
  • Proper storage
  • Fresh daily supply

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Where does your honey come from?

Our honey is sourced from carefully selected regions across India — including Himachal Pradesh, Punjab, Kashmir, and Hanumangarh, Rajasthan. Each variety reflects the unique flora of its origin.

Are your eggs cage-free?

Yes. Our eggs are produced by BV 380 laying hens raised in a cage-free farming system across eight partner farms in the Amritsar region of Punjab, following KVK-recommended practices.

Do you supply retailers?

Absolutely. We work with retail partners and distributors to provide consistent supply of our premium honey and fresh eggs. Contact us to discuss partnership opportunities.

How can I become a distributor?

Reach out through our contact form or WhatsApp. Our team will connect with you to discuss product range, supply schedules, and partnership terms tailored to your business.
